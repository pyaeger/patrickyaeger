Frontier

My principal independent AI research and engineering project: a governed personal AI ecosystem exploring how local models, persistent knowledge, external intelligence, tools, and agents can extend human capability without surrendering human authority.

Frontier is designed around local capability, model-agnostic intelligence, explicit delegation, zero-trust boundaries, provenance, auditability, reversibility, and human judgment. I document selected architecture, experiments, principles, and lessons as the system evolves; dated capability evidence distinguishes governing requirements from verified implementation.

Frontier is governed by The Frontier Constitution, a public charter for sovereign, human-directed personal AI. Version 1.0 is the frozen public release; its constitutional text is licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0. My private knowledge system remains my governing source.
